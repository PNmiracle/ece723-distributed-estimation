What reaches the analyst?

从估计者的位置看问题

ENGLISH　·　朗读
中文　·　对照

Thanks. Let’s take the analyst’s seat. Somewhere in the system there is a pool of N independent observations. We want to estimate a parameter with d coordinates. Each of n machines sees m observations, and together the machines can write at most B bits on a public board. All the analyst gets from the machines is what ends up on that board.

谢谢。现在我们坐到估计者的位置。系统里有一个包含 N 个独立观测的样本池，我们想估计一个 d 维参数。n 台机器各看 m 个观测，合起来最多能在公共黑板上写 B 个比特。机器能告诉估计者的东西，都得体现在黑板记录里。

Here is the question my part answers. Suppose the second machine can read the first bit and choose what to say next. How accurately could any such conversation let us estimate the parameter? I’ll start with separate data, then let two machines share a sample, and finally bring back the bit budget.

我这部分要回答一个问题。假设第二台机器可以先读第一台写的比特，再决定说什么。允许这样互动，估计究竟能有多准？我们先看各自持有独立数据的情形，再让两台机器看到同一个样本，最后把通信预算加回来。

For now, fix the parameter and the random seeds. Give machine A its own block and machine B a separate block. Freeze one complete conversation, say one, zero, one. A spoke first and last. B spoke in the middle. What must have been true of each machine’s data for us to see precisely that conversation?

先固定参数和随机种子。给 A 和 B 各自独立的数据块。现在定格一条完整记录，比如“一、零、一”。A 写第一位和最后一位，B 写中间那一位。要让黑板上恰好出现这三个数，两台机器各自的数据必须满足什么条件？

现场安排　2 分钟画样本池、两台机器与黑板；1 分钟让听众指出哪个箭头承载 B 比特。

Now point to the link whose capacity is measured by B. Is B a bound on the number of data values sampled, or on the bits that appear on that board?

III-C　　 08–23 MIN　　 /　　 论文式 (150)–(159)

Why does a transcript form a rectangle?

定格一条互动记录

ENGLISH　·　朗读
中文　·　对照

A has two checks to pass. Its data must make it write one at the start, and one again after it sees one-zero. B has just one check. After seeing one, its data must make it write zero. If both machines pass, we can replay the conversation bit by bit. The public rule picks the same speakers and stops at the same leaf.

A 的数据要过两关：开头写一，看到“一、零”以后再写一。B 只要过一关：看到“一”以后写零。只要两边都过关，我们就能一位一位地重放整段对话。公开规则会选中相同的发送者，最后停在同一个叶节点。

That is the rectangle. Conditioning on this rectangle restricts each block separately, so the blocks stay independent. Why do we care? Imagine nudging the parameter a little. A score tells us how sensitive a log probability is to that nudge. Let A sub i be the score of block i, and let a sub i be its average among inputs consistent with the conversation. The transcript score is the sum of these averages.

这就是“矩形”。给定这个矩形，只会在每个轴上各自筛选，所以各块仍然条件独立。为什么关心这一点？想象把参数稍微挪一下，得分衡量对数概率对这个变动有多敏感。大写 Aᵢ 是第 i 块的得分，小写 aᵢ 是在符合记录的输入里取的平均。所有小写 aᵢ 加起来，就是记录的得分。

Fisher information takes the squared length of that sum and averages it. For one conversation, two machines’ average scores might point the same way. Across all conversations the cross term vanishes. Conditional independence turns it back into the average product of the raw scores, and independent raw scores have mean zero.

把这个和的长度平方，再求平均，就是费舍尔信息的迹。在某一条记录上，两台机器的平均得分可能同向。但对所有记录取平均，交叉项就消失了。条件独立让我们能把它写回原始得分乘积的期望，而两块原始得分独立、各自均值为零。

板书　·　论文式 (150)原文 ↗　精读 ↗

The target transcript occurs exactly when all the local checklists pass. The converse direction follows by replaying the public schedule one bit at a time.

每张清单都通过，当且仅当目标记录出现。反过来推时，逐位重放公开调度，就能证明清单确实足够。

指读　·　论文式 (151)原文 ↗　精读 ↗

现在分别给三个量起名字：本机通过清单的概率、整条记录的概率，以及通过本机清单的惊讶度。

板书　·　论文式 (152)原文 ↗　精读 ↗

The blocks are independent, so the transcript event factors. Taking a negative logarithm makes its total surprise the sum of local surprises.

各块独立，记录概率就能写成乘积。取负对数，把乘法变成加法，整条记录的惊讶度等于各清单惊讶度之和。

指读　·　论文式 (153)原文 ↗　精读 ↗

Now condition the blocks on this transcript. The joint conditional probability factors into the local conditional probabilities. This is the product posterior behind the rectangle argument.

给定这条记录以后，联合条件分布仍能拆成本机条件分布的乘积。这就是后面消去交叉项所用的独立性。

课堂例题　·　先画矩形，再算零交叉项

现场安排　2 分钟写发送规则；2 分钟在格点上标出目标叶子；3 分钟列出得分表并平均。

Here is a protocol we can replay ourselves. A holds two binary observations, U and V. B holds one binary observation, W. A writes U. If U is zero, stop. If U is one, B writes W. If W is zero, A writes V, and then we stop. For the target one-zero-one, take a moment to write down what A must hold and what B must hold.

我们来重放一个具体协议。A 有两个二元观测 U、V，B 有一个二元观测 W。A 先写 U。U 为零就停；U 为一，B 就写 W。若 W 为零，A 再写 V，然后停。对目标记录一、零、一，请写出 A 的数据和 B 的数据分别要满足什么。

A must hold U equal to one and V equal to one. B must hold W equal to zero. At the fair-coin parameter, and with independent observations, A passes with probability one quarter, B with one half. Their product is one eighth. Now try the leaf zero. Only U is constrained. Both V and W are free. The large leaf and the small leaf are both rectangles. Even a machine that stays silent has a checklist: its whole input space passes.

A 必须 U=1、V=1，B 必须 W=0。在独立公平硬币模型下，A 通过的概率是四分之一，B 是二分之一，两者乘积是八分之一。再试叶子零。此时只有 U 受限，V 和 W 都自由。这个大叶子和刚才的小叶子都是矩形；没有发言的机器，其整个输入空间都通过检查。

Let’s isolate the cancellation with an even smaller experiment. Two machines each reveal one independent Bernoulli observation. At theta equal to one half, a one has score plus two and a zero has score minus two. Fill the four rows in the table. At the one-one leaf, the product is four. At the one-zero leaf it is minus four. So where exactly does zero come from?

再用一个更小的实验单独检查交叉项。两台机器各公开一个独立的伯努利观测。在 θ=1/2 时，一的得分为正二，零的得分为负二。把四行表填完。在一、一叶子上乘积为四，在一、零叶子上为负四。那么零到底出现在哪里？

The results are sixteen, zero, zero, and sixteen. Their average is eight, exactly the sum of the two individual informations, four plus four. This is equation one fifty-nine in a calculation we can see.

III-C　　 23–36 MIN　　 /　　 论文式 (160)–(169)

How does entropy enter Lemma 3?

从局部得分到记录熵

ENGLISH　·　朗读
中文　·　对照

Consider A’s checklist again. If almost every input passes it, learning that A passed tells us very little. If very few inputs pass, their average score can move much further. The event bound from the first half of the talk makes this precise. It charges that shift to the block size and to how surprising it was to pass.

回到 A 的清单。如果几乎所有输入都能通过，知道 A 通过了，就没学到多少东西。如果通过的输入极少，筛选后的平均得分就可能移动得更远。前半场的事件界把这点写成了不等式：移动幅度受块大小和通过清单这件事的罕见程度控制。

Here is the bridge. The probabilities of passing multiply, so their negative logarithms add. The total surprise is exactly minus the log probability of the transcript we actually saw. Its average has a familiar name. It’s entropy. For example, a transcript with probability one quarter carries two bits of surprise. Entropy averages over all transcripts, with their actual probabilities.

连接两边的就是这一步。各清单通过的概率相乘，取负对数后就相加。加起来恰好是实际记录的负对数概率，再取平均，就是熵。比如一条记录的概率为四分之一，它的惊讶度就是两个比特。熵按每条记录实际出现的概率，对这些惊讶度取平均。

Take q to be the largest private block. The quadratic term is controlled because a B-bit protocol has at most two to the B complete leaves. Averaging over the seeds gives Lemma Three. In the sub-Gaussian case the earlier event bound is sharper, and we get the short formula on the second line. We now have a way to price the information in a conversation whenever the inputs are independent.

令 q 为最大私有块的大小。B 比特的协议最多有 2 的 B 次方个完整叶节点，因此平方惊讶度的期望也有上界。最后对种子平均，就得到引理 3。次高斯情形能使用更紧的事件界，得到第二行的简式。这样，只要各输入块独立，我们就知道该怎样给对话的费舍尔信息设上限了。

III-D　　 36–50 MIN　　 /　　 论文式 (170)–(193)

What changes when the samples overlap?

共享样本只计一次

ENGLISH　·　朗读
中文　·　对照

Let’s change just one thing. A sees samples one, two, three. B sees three, four, five. The two copies of sample three are the same measurement. If I apply Lemma Three directly to those two lists, I have used independence where it fails. What could I reveal that would make the lemma available again?

现在只改一件事。A 看样本一、二、三，B 看三、四、五。两边的三号是同一次测量。如果把引理 3 直接用在这两张清单上，我们就错误地假设了独立性。那我可以先公开什么，让引理 3 又能用起来呢？

Give the analyst sample three for free, just for this comparison. Observing the transcript together with extra data can only increase Fisher information. When a lower bound has an extra positive term in the denominator, can we simply cross it out? That would make the claimed lower bound larger. We need another argument. Each comparison gives an inequality for the very same risk, so the strongest of the four applies.

课堂例题　·　反解 Lambert W，再回答收尾问题

现场安排　1 分钟用可手算的数检查反函数；最后 2 分钟留给提问，若无人提问，逐字讲下面两个检验。

We can test the inversion without a calculator. Pick z equal to two plus the log of two. Then exp z is two times e squared. What number multiplied by its own exponential gives that? Two does. The ceiling on t is therefore two, so the floor on D is d over two m. This is a check on the direction of the inequality, using a convenient test value of z.

不用计算器也能检查反解。举一个便于手算的 z=2+ln 2，此时 e 的 z 次方等于 2e²。哪个数乘以自身的指数会得到它？就是二。所以 t 的上限是二，D 的下限是 d/(2m)。这里特意选了一个好算的 z 来检查方向。

Could shared observations make the bound look weaker? Yes. A larger overlap count credits more information to the enlarged experiment. That gives a larger information ceiling and hence a weaker risk floor. It does not by itself answer which sampling design attains the best risk.

共享样本变多，为什么下界看起来反而变弱？因为扩大实验给共享值记了更多信息，信息上限更大，推出的风险下限就可能更低。单看这个式子，还不能比较哪种抽样设计能做到最低风险。

And where did we use interaction? At the rectangle step. The next speaker can react to what has already been written. Once we fix a complete public history, those decisions are known. That is why the proof covers the interactive protocols in this model.

那么互动具体在哪里被处理了？就在矩形那一步。下一位发言者可以根据已经公开的内容行动；一旦固定完整的公开历史，这些决定就都确定了。因此证明覆盖了模型中允许的交互协议。
