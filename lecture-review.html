一页速览At a glance

项目怎么变了What changed

个人项目取消。全班一起研读、核查和修订老师用 AI 生成的工作论文，目标是投稿，参与者共同署名。

Individual projects are cancelled. The class will study, check and revise one AI-generated working paper together, aiming to submit it with everyone as co-authors.

问题是什么The problem

$n$ 台机器从同一个大小为 $N$ 的样本池各抽 $m$ 个样本（可能重叠），每台只能发 $k$ 比特。融合中心最好能把 $\theta$ 估计到多准？

$n$ machines each draw $m$ samples from one shared pool of size $N$, so their samples may overlap. Each machine can send only $k$ bits. How well can the fusion center estimate $\theta$?

主要结果Main result

下界（BB 协议）和上界（NI 协议）是四项取最大，只有第四项相差 $\bar r$。所以在大多数区间里，交互不会改善误差的量级。

The lower bound (BB) and upper bound (NI) are both a max of four terms, and only the fourth term differs, by $\bar r$. In most regimes, interaction does not improve the order of the error.

你的两项任务Your two tasks

① 读懂并核查第 III 节，下周汇报；② 用 AI 尝试闭合临界窗口里的 gap，部分进展也写成 PDF 分享。

① Understand and check Section III, then present it next week. ② Try to close the gap in the critical window with AI, and write up partial progress as a PDF to share.

$$\underbrace{c\,L}_{\text{Sec. III}}\;\le\; R^*_{\mathrm{BB}} \;\le\; R^*_{\mathrm{NI}} \;\le\; \underbrace{C\,U}_{\text{Sec. IV}}$$ $$L = d\max\Big\{\tfrac1N,\ \tfrac1{nm},\ a_B,\ \tfrac{1}{m(1+s_+)+\bar r}\Big\},\qquad U = d\max\Big\{\tfrac1N,\ \tfrac1{nm},\ a_B,\ \tfrac{1}{m(1+s_+)}\Big\}$$

$B=nk,\ a_B=2^{-2B/d},\ s=\tfrac{B}{d}-\tfrac12\log_2 m,\ s_+=\max(s,0)$；$\bar r$ 为至少被两台处理器看到的不同样本的期望数。 $\bar r$ is the expected number of distinct samples seen by at least two processors.

2 · 公共样本池、重叠与通信协议Common pool, overlap and communication protocols

📝总结Summary

分布 $P_\theta$ 的参数 $\theta\in\mathbb{R}^d$ 未知，$d$ 固定，例如方差已知的高斯 $\mathcal N(\theta,\sigma^2)$。系统按以下流程工作：

The distribution $P_\theta$ has an unknown parameter $\theta\in\mathbb{R}^d$ with $d$ fixed, e.g. a Gaussian $\mathcal N(\theta,\sigma^2)$ with known variance. The system works as follows:

  • 公共池里有 $N$ 个 i.i.d. 样本 $X_1,\dots,X_N$。The common pool holds $N$ i.i.d. samples $X_1,\dots,X_N$.
  • $n$ 个处理单元各自从 $\binom{N}{m}$ 个子集中均匀随机选一个，下载 $m$ 个样本；各单元的选择相互独立，所以可能重叠。Each of $n$ units picks one of the $\binom{N}{m}$ subsets uniformly at random and downloads its $m$ samples. Units choose independently, so their samples can overlap.
  • 每个单元把观测压缩成至多 $k$ 比特，发给融合中心（fusion center），由它输出 $\hat\theta$。Each unit compresses its observations into at most $k$ bits and sends them to the fusion center, which outputs $\hat\theta$.
  • 抽样设计 $S=(S_1,\dots,S_n)$，即谁下载了哪些索引，对所有单元和融合中心公开；样本的内容只有下载者自己知道。The sampling design $S=(S_1,\dots,S_n)$, i.e. which indices each unit downloaded, is known to every unit and to the fusion center. The sample values are known only to the units that downloaded them.
  • 允许随机化：公共随机种子 $R_0$ 对所有单元和融合中心可见，各单元另有私有随机种子。形式化允许这些随机性，但不代表一定用得上。Randomization is allowed: a common random seed $R_0$ visible to all units and the fusion center, plus a private seed at each unit. The formulation allows them; that doesn't mean they are needed.

两种协议。非交互（NI）：每台一次性发出自己的 $k$ 比特。黑板（BB）：比特一位一位写在公开的“黑板”上，下一位由谁写取决于已写下的历史，但每台累计仍至多 $k$ 比特。BB 包含 NI，所以 BB 更强。可论文表明，对论文研究的所有情形（除 gap 区间外），两者在量级上一样强：下界对 BB 成立，上界由 NI 达到，两界同阶。

Two protocols. Non-interactive (NI): each unit sends its $k$ bits in one shot. Blackboard (BB): bits are written one at a time on a public "blackboard", the next writer depends on the history so far, and each unit still writes at most $k$ bits. BB contains NI, so it is stronger. Yet the paper shows the two are equally strong in scaling for every case it studies (outside the gap regime): the lower bound holds for BB and NI achieves the upper bound, and the two bounds have the same order.

💡通俗解释In plain words

想象一个有 $N$ 本书的图书馆，$n$ 个学生各自随机借 $m$ 本，可能借到同一本。借书登记表大家都能看到，所以你知道“他借了 2、3、8 号”，但不知道那几本书写了什么。每个学生只能给老师发一条很短的短信（$k$ 比特）。登记表很有用：如果我们借的书完全一样，就该分工，各报一部分；如果完全不重叠，就可以各报各的。黑板协议像课堂轮流发言：每人说一个字，大家都听得见，再决定下一个谁说。

Picture a library with $N$ books. Each of $n$ students randomly borrows $m$ of them, and two students may borrow the same book. The borrowing log is public, so you know "he took books 2, 3 and 8" but not what is inside them. Each student can send the teacher only one short text ($k$ bits). The log is useful: if we borrowed exactly the same books, we should split up who reports what; if there is no overlap, each can report independently. The blackboard protocol is like taking turns in class: each person says one word, everyone hears it, and then the group decides who speaks next.

📌可积累的点Takeaways

  • 形式化要严谨：把所有隐含假设写清楚（谁知道什么、什么是随机的），否则无法做严格分析。Be rigorous in the formulation: state every implicit assumption (who knows what, what is random), or rigorous analysis is impossible.
  • 证明策略：对更强的模型（BB）证下界，用更弱的模型（NI）达到上界。两者匹配时，同时说明了“交互没用”。Proof strategy: prove the lower bound for the stronger model (BB) and achieve the upper bound with the weaker one (NI). When they match, this also shows that interaction doesn't help.
  • 要区分“形式化允许”和“方案需要”，随机性就是一例。Distinguish what the formulation allows from what a scheme needs; randomness is an example.

❓自测Self-check

为什么“BB 的下界 + NI 的上界同阶”就能说明交互没有帮助？Why do "a BB lower bound + an NI upper bound of the same order" show interaction doesn't help?

NI 是 BB 的特例，所以 $R^*_{\mathrm{BB}}\le R^*_{\mathrm{NI}}$。又有 $cL\le R^*_{\mathrm{BB}}$ 和 $R^*_{\mathrm{NI}}\le CU$。若 $L\asymp U$，两者就被夹在同一量级，交互最多只改进常数。

NI is a special case of BB, so $R^*_{\mathrm{BB}}\le R^*_{\mathrm{NI}}$. Combined with $cL\le R^*_{\mathrm{BB}}$ and $R^*_{\mathrm{NI}}\le CU$, if $L\asymp U$ both are squeezed to the same order, so interaction can improve at most the constant.

知道别人下载的索引（但不知道内容）有什么用？What good is knowing others' indices without their values?

它能让大家协调。重叠的样本是同一份观测，不能当成两份独立数据；知道重叠情况后，可以避免重复发送同样的信息，或者分工编码。

It enables coordination. An overlapping sample is a single observation and must not be counted as two independent ones. Knowing the overlap lets units avoid sending the same information twice, or split up the encoding.

3 · 平方损失、最坏情况与 minimaxSquared loss, worst case and minimax

📝总结Summary

误差用平方损失 $\mathbb{E}_\theta\|\hat\theta-\theta\|_2^2$ 衡量。固定 $\theta$ 时，$\hat\theta$ 仍然是随机的，随机性有四个来源：数据、抽样设计（下载了哪 $m$ 个）、私有随机性、公共随机性，期望对它们全部取平均。

Error is measured by the squared loss $\mathbb{E}_\theta\|\hat\theta-\theta\|_2^2$. With $\theta$ fixed, $\hat\theta$ is still random, and the randomness has four sources: the data, the sampling design (which $m$ samples were downloaded), private randomness, and common randomness. The expectation averages over all of them.

但“对每个 $\theta$ 都最小化”是不良定义的。反例：一个融合中心不看数据、总是输出 $\theta_0$，另一个总是输出 $\theta_1$。前者在 $\theta=\theta_0$ 处误差为零，后者在 $\theta_1$ 处为零，谁也无法支配（dominate）对方。所以要比较最坏情况表现，并寻找最坏情况最好的方案：

But "minimize it for every $\theta$" is not well defined. Counterexample: one fusion center ignores the data and always outputs $\theta_0$; another always outputs $\theta_1$. The first has zero error at $\theta=\theta_0$, the second at $\theta_1$, and neither dominates the other. So we compare worst-case performance and look for the scheme whose worst case is best:

$$R^* \;=\; \inf_{\text{protocol},\,\hat\theta}\ \sup_{\theta\in\Theta}\ \mathbb{E}_\theta\big\|\hat\theta-\theta\big\|_2^2$$

这一段还讲了汇报要求：不要逐行照抄，要在细节和全局之间取得平衡；目标是投稿，证明错了要自己负责，所以要仔细核查；某段看不懂就换个角度再问 AI。

This segment also covered presentation expectations: don't copy line by line, and balance details against the big picture. The goal is submission and you are accountable if a proof is wrong, so check carefully; if an explanation doesn't land, ask the AI again from a different angle.

💡通俗解释In plain words

就像选一个“没有短板”的学生，而不是某一科满分、别的科不及格的偏科生。$\sup_\theta$ 先找出每个方案最差的那一科，$\inf$ 再挑出最差成绩最好的那个方案。

It's like picking the student with no weak subject rather than one who aces one subject and fails the rest. $\sup_\theta$ finds each scheme's worst subject; $\inf$ then picks the scheme whose worst score is best.

📌可积累的点Takeaways

  • 参数未知时，不存在一致最优的估计器，这就是 minimax（或 Bayes）准则存在的原因。When the parameter is unknown, no estimator is uniformly best. That is why minimax (or Bayes) criteria exist.
  • “常数估计器”是检验一个准则是否合理的经典反例。The constant estimator is the classic counterexample for testing whether a criterion makes sense.
  • 理论研究最难的往往是形式化本身。有了 AI，求解变得容易了，但仍需要理解解法。In theory research the hardest part is often the formulation. AI makes solving easier, but you still need to understand the solution.

4 · Converse、Achievability 与四项界Converse, achievability and the four-term bounds

📝总结Summary

证明一个基本极限（fundamental limit）要两步：Achievability，构造一个方案达到这个性能；Converse，证明任何方案都无法超越它。只有可达性，说明这个性能能达到，但不一定最好；只有逆定理，说明不能更好，但可能根本达不到。两者合在一起才是完整的故事。

Establishing a fundamental limit takes two parts. Achievability: construct a scheme that reaches the performance. Converse: prove no scheme can beat it. Achievability alone says the performance is reachable, not that it's the best; a converse alone says nothing can do better, not that the limit is reachable. Together they give the complete story.

$$L = d\max\Big\{\tfrac1N,\ \tfrac1{nm},\ a_B,\ \tfrac{1}{m(1+s_+)+\bar r}\Big\}\quad\text{(Sec. III, any BB protocol)}$$ $$U = d\max\Big\{\tfrac1N,\ \tfrac1{nm},\ a_B,\ \tfrac{1}{m(1+s_+)}\Big\}\quad\text{(Sec. IV, an NI scheme)}$$ $$B=nk,\qquad a_B=2^{-2B/d},\qquad s=\frac{B}{d}-\frac12\log_2 m,\qquad s_+=\max(s,0)$$
项Term瓶颈Bottleneck直觉Intuition
$d/N$公共池大小Pool size融合中心直接拿到全部 $N$ 个样本时（集中式）的经典速率。$d$ 越大要估的数越多，$N$ 越大越准。The classical centralized rate when the fusion center sees all $N$ samples. Larger $d$ means more numbers to estimate; larger $N$ means more accuracy.
$d/(nm)$总访问量Total access假设没有比特限制、各单元拿到的样本也全都独立，最多也只有 $nm$ 个样本。因为重叠，$nm$ 可以大于 $N$，所以两项都要保留。Even with no bit limit and all accessed samples independent, there are at most $nm$ samples. Because of overlap, $nm$ can exceed $N$, so both terms are kept.
$d\,a_B$比特瓶颈Bit budget总比特 $B$ 平均分给 $d$ 个坐标，每维 $B/d$ 比特。量化理论：每维 $b$ 比特，平方误差约 $2^{-2b}$。The total budget $B$ is shared across $d$ coordinates, giving $B/d$ bits each. Quantization theory: $b$ bits per coordinate give squared error about $2^{-2b}$.
$\dfrac{d}{m(1+s_+)}$定位 + 细化Localization + refinement单台机器有 $m$ 个样本，误差约 $1/m$。要把这个精度传出去，每维约需 $\tfrac12\log_2 m$ 比特；多出来的 $s$ 比特（surplus）用于进一步细化。One machine with $m$ samples gets error about $1/m$. Transmitting that precision costs about $\tfrac12\log_2 m$ bits per dimension; the extra $s$ bits (the surplus) refine further.

为什么定位需要 $\tfrac12\log_2 m$ 比特？标准差约为 $1/\sqrt m$；要让量化步长 $2^{-b}$ 达到这个尺度，需要 $2^{-b}\approx m^{-1/2}$，即 $b\approx\tfrac12\log_2 m$。

Why $\tfrac12\log_2 m$ bits for localization? The standard deviation is about $1/\sqrt m$. For the quantization step $2^{-b}$ to reach that scale we need $2^{-b}\approx m^{-1/2}$, i.e. $b\approx\tfrac12\log_2 m$.

第四项最难：作为上界，“只用一台机器”就可以达到；但作为下界，不能假定对方只用一台机器，下界必须对所有 BB 协议成立。只对某个具体方案成立的下界没有意义。下界里多出的 $\bar r$ 记录的是“共享样本”带来的额外信息，这使现有下界变弱。

The fourth term is the hardest. As an upper bound, "use one machine" achieves it. As a lower bound, you can't assume the opponent uses only one machine: it must hold for every BB protocol, and a lower bound for one particular scheme is useless. The extra $\bar r$ in the lower bound accounts for the information carried by shared samples, and it is what makes the current lower bound weaker.

💡通俗解释In plain words

把它想成一根水管上的四个阀门：池子大小、总访问量、比特带宽、单机精度。水流（估计精度）由最窄的那个阀门决定，所以误差取四项的 max。第四项说的是：先花 $\tfrac12\log_2 m$ 比特把答案“定位”到一台机器的精度，剩下的比特再用来“细化”。

Think of four valves on one pipe: pool size, total access, bit bandwidth and single-machine precision. The flow (estimation accuracy) is set by the narrowest valve, so the error is the max of the four terms. The fourth term says: spend $\tfrac12\log_2 m$ bits to "localize" the answer to one machine's precision, then use the leftover bits to "refine" it.

📌可积累的点Takeaways

  • Achievability + Converse = 基本极限。这是信息论和统计学习里的通用结构。Achievability + converse = fundamental limit. This structure is standard across information theory and statistical learning.
  • 四项里前三项基本是经典结果：$d/N$ 是教科书级的参数估计，$a_B$ 来自量化。真正新的是第四项和 $\bar r$。The first three terms are essentially classical: $d/N$ is textbook parameter estimation and $a_B$ comes from quantization. What's new is the fourth term and $\bar r$.
  • 下界必须是普适的（universal）：对所有协议成立，不能对方案做任何假设。A lower bound must be universal: it holds for all protocols and assumes nothing about the scheme.

❓自测Self-check

为什么 $d/(nm)$ 有时比 $d/N$ 小，两项都要保留？Why can $d/(nm)$ be smaller than $d/N$, so both terms are needed?

因为样本会重叠：单元多、每台下载得多时，$nm$ 可以远大于 $N$。这时真正独立的样本最多 $N$ 个，$d/N$ 是更紧的限制；反过来 $nm < N$ 时，$d/(nm)$ 更紧。

Because samples overlap: with many units each downloading a lot, $nm$ can far exceed $N$. Then there are at most $N$ independent samples and $d/N$ is the tighter limit. When $nm < N$, $d/(nm)$ is tighter.

$s_+=0$ 时第四项变成什么？代表什么？What does the fourth term become when $s_+=0$, and what does it mean?

变成 $d/m$，即只用一台机器的 $m$ 个样本时的误差。比特刚够“定位”，没有余量做细化。

It becomes $d/m$, the error from a single machine's $m$ samples. There are just enough bits to localize, with nothing left for refinement.

5 · 临界窗口与开放问题The critical window and the open problem

📝总结Summary

上下界只在第四项相差 $\bar r$。只有 $s_+>0$ 并不足以说明两界不同阶，gap 只出现在论文第 VI 节标出的临界窗口：

The bounds differ only by $\bar r$ in the fourth term. $s_+>0$ alone does not make the bounds differ in order; the gap appears only in the critical window marked in Section VI of the paper:

$$s\to\infty,\qquad s=o(n),\qquad ms\ \ll\ N\ \ll\ \frac{n^2 m}{s}$$
  • $N\lesssim ms$：池子本身是瓶颈，两界都是 $d/N$ 量级，问题退化为集中式。$N\lesssim ms$: the pool itself is the bottleneck, both bounds are of order $d/N$, and the problem reduces to the centralized one.
  • $N\gtrsim n^2m/s$：共享项可以控制住，两界都是 $d/(ms)$ 量级，相当于已解决的独立样本问题（课上提到 Cai 和 Wei 的工作）。$N\gtrsim n^2m/s$: the shared term is under control, both bounds are of order $d/(ms)$, and this matches the already-solved independent-sample problem (the class mentioned work by Cai and Wei).
  • 中间段：重叠既不能忽略、也不占主导，这正是论文的新意所在，目前仍未解决。In between, the overlap is neither negligible nor dominant. This is where the paper's novelty lies, and it is still unsolved.

三种可能：① 下界是紧的，那就要找更好的方案把上界压下来；② 上界是紧的，那就要改进下界；③ 两个都不紧，需要两边一起收紧，在中间相遇。老师认为这个问题“不是没希望”，需要的是一些技术上的创新，没有超出现有技术的能力。建议多换模型、来回交叉讨论，并把部分进展整理成 PDF 分享。

Three possibilities: ① the lower bound is tight, so a better scheme must bring the upper bound down; ② the upper bound is tight, so the lower bound must improve; ③ neither is tight, and both must move to meet in the middle. The professor thinks the problem is "not hopeless": it needs some technical innovation, not something beyond current techniques. His advice: try different models, pass ideas back and forth between them, and write partial progress up as a PDF to share.

💡通俗解释In plain words

池子太小，大家借的书几乎一样，就等于一个人在估计；池子极大，几乎没人借到同一本，就是老问题。偏偏池子“不大不小”时，有些书被好几个人同时借到，这些重复既不能当没看见，也没有多到决定一切，现有工具在这里还不够用。

If the pool is tiny, everyone borrows nearly the same books and it's like one person estimating alone. If it's huge, almost no two people get the same book, which is the old, solved problem. When the pool is "medium-sized", some books are borrowed by several people at once: the duplicates can't be ignored, but they don't decide everything either, and existing tools aren't enough here.

📌可积累的点Takeaways

  • 判断一篇论文的新意：看哪些结果能从已有结果“推出来”，哪些只在新模型里才出现。To judge a paper's novelty, ask which results follow from known work and which only appear in the new model.
  • 上下界之间有 gap 时，先想清楚三种可能，再决定攻哪一边。When upper and lower bounds have a gap, lay out the three possibilities before deciding which side to attack.
  • 用 AI 做研究：结果取决于提示词和对话历史；不同模型的结果互相喂给对方，能产生多样性。Doing research with AI: results depend on the prompt and the conversation history, and feeding one model's output to another creates diversity.

为什么说没有公共池时就不会出现这个 gap？Why does this gap not arise without a common pool?

6 · Fisher 信息、正则条件与投稿讨论Fisher information, regularity conditions and submission

📝总结Summary

正则条件（regularity conditions），比如 Lipschitz 连续、连续可微、可交换求导与积分次序，是让证明成立的技术条件，但不是重点。实际研究中，往往先写出推导，再把用到的条件收集起来写进定理陈述。汇报时在用到的地方点一下即可，甚至可以假设很强的条件（如无穷可微），对高斯均值这些条件都自然成立。

Regularity conditions (Lipschitz continuity, continuous differentiability, swapping derivative and integral) are technical conditions that make the proof work, but they aren't the focus. In practice you write the derivation first, then collect the conditions you used into the theorem statement. In the talk, just mention them where they are used; you may even assume very strong conditions (e.g. infinitely differentiable), and for the Gaussian mean they all hold anyway.

重点是讲清 score 与 Fisher 信息怎样连到 minimax 风险：

The focus is how the score and Fisher information connect to the minimax risk:

$$\text{score: } \nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(X),\qquad I(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_\theta\!\big[\nabla_\theta\log p_\theta(X)\,\nabla_\theta\log p_\theta(X)^{\!\top}\big]$$ $$\text{Gaussian: } X\sim\mathcal N(\theta,\sigma^2 I_d)\ \Rightarrow\ \nabla_\theta\log p_\theta(X)=\frac{X-\theta}{\sigma^2},\quad I(\theta)=\frac{I_d}{\sigma^2},\quad \frac{\operatorname{tr}\big(I(\theta)^{-1}\big)}{N}=\frac{d\,\sigma^2}{N}$$

最后一式正是 $d/N$ 的来源。在第 III 节，融合中心只看到通信记录，所以要用“给定记录下的条件 score”的 Fisher 信息，再用 van Trees 不等式把信息的上限转成风险的下限。

The last expression is exactly where $d/N$ comes from. In Section III the fusion center sees only the transcript, so we use the Fisher information of the score conditioned on the transcript, and the van Trees inequality turns an upper bound on information into a lower bound on risk.

关于稿件：老师要的是期刊稿，初版不到 10 页，没有附录，他逐节扩写到现在的篇幅。他也坦言：如果闭合不了 gap，这篇工作更像增量式的，即把已知技巧搬到新模型上。即便在 $N$ 很大的区间，也要严格论证“碰撞”（重叠）的小概率不影响渐近行为，这需要分析，但不是主要创新点。

On the manuscript: he asked for a journal paper. The first draft was under 10 pages with no appendix, and he expanded it section by section. He was candid: without closing the gap, the work is more incremental, adapting known techniques to a new model. Even when $N$ is large, you must rigorously show that the small chance of a collision (overlap) doesn't affect the asymptotics. That takes analysis but isn't the main novelty.

💡通俗解释In plain words

正则条件就像“路面平整”的假设，保证你能放心求导、交换积分次序。汇报该讲的是车怎么开，不是路面的施工规范。Fisher 信息衡量数据对 $\theta$ 的微小变化有多“敏感”：越敏感，信息越多，误差下限大约是 $1/\text{信息量}$。

Regularity conditions are like assuming the road is paved, so you can safely differentiate and swap integrals. The talk should be about how to drive the car, not the road-construction code. Fisher information measures how sensitive the data are to a tiny change in $\theta$: more sensitivity means more information, and the error floor is roughly $1/\text{information}$.

📌可积累的点Takeaways

  • 真实的证明流程：先推导，再收集需要的假设写进定理。定理不是先列好条件再去证的。How proofs really get written: derive first, then collect the needed assumptions into the theorem. Conditions aren't listed up front.
  • 下界的经典链条：score → Fisher 信息 → Cramér–Rao / van Trees → 风险下界。The classic lower-bound chain: score → Fisher information → Cramér–Rao / van Trees → risk lower bound.
  • “增量式工作”（incremental work）是审稿时常见的负面评价，指把已知结果搬到稍有不同的模型上。"Incremental work" is a common reviewer criticism: reproducing known results in a slightly different model.

定义 score 为什么需要正则条件？Why does defining the score need regularity conditions?
